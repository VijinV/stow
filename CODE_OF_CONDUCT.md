# Code of conduct

Stow welcomes contributors and users of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat people with respect, discuss ideas on their merits, and give constructive feedback. Respect people’s names, pronouns, time, and privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone’s private information without permission are unacceptable. These standards apply to project issues, pull requests, and other spaces used for official project work.

If you experience or witness misconduct, contact [the project maintainer](https://github.com/VijinV) through an available private channel. Use [GitHub’s abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for abuse on GitHub. Share only the details needed to review the incident and avoid posting private information in an issue.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or block accounts when needed to protect the community. Reports should be handled as privately as the circumstances allow.
